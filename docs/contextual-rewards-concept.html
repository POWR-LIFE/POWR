● POWR · Product Concept

Contextual Sponsored Rewards

Reward partners pay to surface their discount the moment a user is physically in a high-intent context — on a golf course, mid-walk, 7am near a café. Invisible on the map. First-party. Presence-attributed.

Not a map feature · not third-party ads Sells targeting, not data Reuses ~80% of shipped infra New revenue lever on existing partners

What keeps it on the right side of the line

06Guardrails

🛡️

First-party only

Your partners, your catalogue, your data. Nothing leaves POWR — the App Store / privacy landmine of "selling location to advertisers" never applies.

🎁

Ship as a reward, never an ad

"Here's 50 points + an offer" reads as a perk. "Here's an ad" reads as surveillance. Same backend, opposite reception.

🚦

Pace it hard

Reuse the notification discipline already tuned: capped, daytime-clamped, deduped. One spammy week churns users.
